Level 1 is the frontline support agent, who responds to queries within 24 hours. If a case is unresolved after 24 hours, it moves to Level 2, the team lead, who responds within 48 hours. Level 3 is the customer experience manager, who handles cases open for more than 3 days, shipments delayed more than 7 days, or refunds above 10000 rupees. Fraud or safety complaints skip directly to Level 3.
